## The service

Deasy lets you keep tasks and events, add them by typing or voice, receive reminders, and optionally sync events with the calendar on your iPhone. Features can change. The app needs a network connection for sign-in, saving plans, weather, and purchases.

## Deasy Pro

Deasy Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold by Apple.

- The price, billing period, and what Pro includes are shown in the App Store purchase sheet before you confirm.
- Payment is charged to your Apple ID.
- The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- You can cancel in the iPhone Settings app under Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deasy’s subscription row opens that page. Canceling stops renewal. It does not refund the current period and it does not delete your account.
- Restore a purchase from Settings in Deasy with the same Apple ID.
- Any unused portion of a free trial, if Apple offers one, is forfeited when you buy a subscription, where Apple’s rules say so.

## Account removal

You can remove the sign-in account in Settings. See the Privacy Policy for what remains stored and how to ask for it to be deleted.
