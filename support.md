Common topics
Reminders & notifications — Check Settings → Notifications for Deasy, and that Morning Briefing / reminder times are set in the app.
Location / arrive alerts — Needs Location set to Always for alerts while the app is closed.
Subscriptions (Deasy Pro) — Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple.
Backup / export — Use Settings in the app to export a backup or calendar (.ics) file.
Privacy — See our Privacy Policy linked on the App Store product page and inside the app (Settings).
Restore purchases
Open Deasy → Settings → Deasy Pro → Restore purchases.
