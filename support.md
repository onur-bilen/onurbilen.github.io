# Deasy Support

Thanks for using Deasy.

## Contact

For help, feedback, privacy requests, or subscription questions, email **[onurbilen@gmail.com](mailto:onurbilen@gmail.com)**.

Please include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- A short description of what happened
- Screenshots if they help

We usually reply within a few business days.

## Common topics

- **Sign-in.** Deasy uses Sign in with Apple or Google. If account removal asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign in, and try once more.
- **Voice.** The microphone and speech recognition permissions are required to turn speech into a plan. You confirm the plan before it is saved.
- **Reminders.** Allow notifications for Deasy. Reminders are scheduled on the iPhone.
- **Saved places.** Adding a place can ask for location. Arrival reminders, about 1 km from a saved place, need **Always** so they can fire while Deasy is closed. Deasy does not track your location continuously.
- **Calendar.** If calendar sync is on, Deasy can add its events to a calendar on the iPhone and remove a plan when the matching calendar event is deleted.
- **Deasy Pro.** The price and period are shown by Apple before you pay. Restore a purchase from Settings. Cancel in the iPhone Settings app under Apple ID → Subscriptions. Canceling stops the next renewal. It does not delete your account.
- **Remove account.** Settings includes account removal. That deletes the sign-in. Email us if you also want the plans and saved places stored for that account deleted.
